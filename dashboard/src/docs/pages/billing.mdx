# Billing & Plans

## Pricing Model

Moneat uses a GB-based pricing model. Your usage is measured by the volume of event data ingested and stored. Each plan includes a monthly data allowance, and you can add additional capacity as needed.

## Plan Tiers

| Plan | Price | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Basic error monitoring, limited event volume, core features. |
| **Pro** | Starting price/month | Higher event limits, longer retention, integrations. |
| **Team** | Custom pricing | On-call management, SAML SSO, SSO enforcement, higher data volumes. |
| **Business** | Custom pricing | Extended retention, priority support. |

:::info[Visit the [pricing page](https://moneat.io) for current pricing and plan comparison.]
:::

:::tip[Self-hosted SSO]
OIDC SSO is available on all self-hosted deployments at no cost. SAML SSO and SSO enforcement require an enterprise license.
:::

## Usage Tracking

Monitor your data usage in real-time from **Settings & Billing**. The usage dashboard shows:

- **Current period usage** - Data ingested this billing cycle
- **Usage breakdown** - By project, event type, and day
- **Quota remaining** - Capacity left
- **Historical trends** - Usage patterns over previous months

Retention is configured per plan and can differ by signal type, including APM traces.

## On-Call Seats

On-call management is billed per seat. Each team member who participates in on-call schedules requires a seat. You can add or remove seats at any time from the billing settings.

## Pay-As-You-Go

### Budget Limits

If you exceed your plan's included data, pay-as-you-go pricing applies. You can set a monthly budget limit to cap your spending. When the budget is reached, new events are dropped until the next billing cycle.

:::warning[Avoid surprises]
Set a pay-as-you-go budget limit to prevent unexpected charges. You'll receive alerts as you approach your budget threshold.
:::

## Managing Your Subscription

All billing operations are handled through **Settings & Billing**:

- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Update payment method
- View and download invoices
- Adjust on-call seat count
- Set pay-as-you-go budget limits

Payment processing is handled securely through Stripe. Moneat does not store your credit card information directly.
